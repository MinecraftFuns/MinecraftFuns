> Earth is destroyed to make way for a hyperspace bypass built where it used to stand. The protagonist, Arthur Dent, survives because he has a friend named Ford Prefect. This friend appears to be an out-of-work actor, but is actually an alien, a researcher sent to Earth by the famous book The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. The two set out on an adventure across the galaxy, helped only by the boundless wisdom contained in The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy.
>
> Along the way, they encounter a group of very interesting companions:
>
> * Zaphod Beeblebrox: a two-headed, three-armed galactic outlaw, whose other identity is President of the Galaxy.
> * Trillian: Zaphod's companion, and the only human survivor from Earth besides Arthur Dent. In fact, Arthur knew Trillian before and once tried to hit on her, but without success.
> * Marvin: a genius robot, extremely paranoid, extremely depressed, and extremely talkative.
>
> Together these characters form a small team, and they will uncover a shocking secret…
